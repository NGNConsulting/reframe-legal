Privacy Policy

Reframe · Last updated 28 July 2026

Reframe (“the app”) is operated by NGN Consulting Corp. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how long we keep it. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than legal boilerplate.

Reframe helps you understand messages in your relationship. That means you may share genuinely sensitive material with us — private conversations, feelings, and details about someone who is not a user of this app. We take that seriously, and the rules below are enforced in our code, not just stated here.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Email address and password To create and secure your account. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store your plain-text password.
Screenshots you upload To read the conversation you want analysed. See “Screenshots are deleted within 24 hours” below.
Message text and thread context extracted from those screenshots, or typed by you To produce the analysis, and to power the memory and pattern features that look across your history.
Analysis results (interpretation, tone score, suggested replies) So you can revisit past analyses and so the app can track patterns over time.
Milestones you add (label, date, importance, reminder preference) To remind you about them and to give the AI relevant context.
Notification token and preferences To send the push notifications you have enabled. You can turn these off at any time.
Subscription status To unlock paid features. Payment itself is handled by Google Play — see section 3.

We do not collect: your contacts, your location, your device identifiers for advertising, your other apps, or your general phone activity. Reframe contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information about other people

Screenshots you upload will usually contain messages written by someone else. You are responsible for having a legitimate reason to share that content with us. We use it only to generate your analysis, we never contact that person, and we never build a profile of them. If they contact us to ask what we hold about them, we will act on the deletion rules in section 5.

2. How we use your information

We use it to operate the app: to authenticate you, generate analyses, remember relevant context, send reminders you asked for, enforce subscription limits, and keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use your conversations to train AI models — see section 3.

We use a small number of processors to run the service. Each receives only what it needs.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to, or where necessary to protect someone’s safety or our legal rights. If our business is ever sold or transferred, your data may transfer with it; we will tell you before that happens.

4. Screenshots are deleted within 24 hours

This is a core rule of the product, not a courtesy. Every uploaded screenshot image is permanently deleted from storage within 24 hours of upload. A cleanup job runs every hour and again whenever our server restarts, so a restart cannot extend the deadline. Orphaned files — uploads whose analysis never completed — are swept on the same schedule.

The text extracted from a screenshot, and the analysis produced from it, are kept in your account so that your history, memory and pattern features work. If you do not want that text retained, delete the analysis or your account.

5. How long we keep things, and how to delete them

DataRetention
Screenshot imagesDeleted within 24 hours, automatically and permanently
Analyses, extracted text, milestones, accountKept until you delete them or close your account
Subscription recordsKept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes

You can delete individual milestones in the app at any time.

To delete your account and everything in it, open the app and go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, analyses, extracted message text, milestones, patterns, snapshots and any screenshot still within its 24-hour window are all erased at once. There is nothing to pay and no reason you need to give.

If you can no longer sign in, email firefly.express.sales@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the subject line “Delete my Reframe account”, and we will do it for you within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, export it, or object to how we use it. Residents of California, the EEA and the UK have these rights by statute; we extend the same handling to everyone. To exercise any of them, email us at the address above. We will not charge you or treat you differently for asking.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our infrastructure providers. Access to production data is restricted, database-level row security ensures one account cannot read another’s data, and our API is rate-limited against abuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not treat that as an excuse: if a breach affects your data, we will notify you promptly.

8. Children

Reframe is not intended for anyone under 18, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

We operate from the United States, and our providers process data in the United States and other countries. If you use Reframe from outside the US, you are sending your information to the US, where data protection law may differ from your own.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app before the change takes effect. Continuing to use Reframe after that means you accept the updated policy.
